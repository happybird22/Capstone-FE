Frequently Asked Questions

Session Journal

What is Session Journal?

A companion app for tabletop RPG groups to log session notes, track NPCs and places, and keep a shared party inventory — so nobody has to remember what happened three sessions ago.

What's the difference between Player and Game Master?

You pick a role when you register. Game Masters create parties and share an invite code with their table. Players join an existing party using that code. Both roles can write and manage their own session notes.

How do I join a party?

Ask your Game Master for their party's invite code, then enter it on the Join Party form. Only a Game Master can create a new party.

Who can see my session notes?

You choose when you write each note: Private (just you), Whole Party (everyone in your party), or Someone specific (only the party members you pick).

What is the Party Bank?

A shared inventory for your party — items, quantities, and value — that updates live for every member as anyone adds, edits, or removes something.

Is my data private?

Yes — no ads, no analytics, no selling your data. See the full Privacy Policy for details on what's collected and how it's used.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes — Session Journal is also available as part of the Dungeons Not Dating app, currently available in the US and Canada.

How do I delete my account?

See our account deletion page — email us from your account's address and we'll process the request within 30 days.

Is Session Journal free?

Yes, there's no charge to use it.
