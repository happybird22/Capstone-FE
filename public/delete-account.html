Delete Your Account

Session Journal

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open Session Journal, go to the Account tab, and tap Delete My Account under Danger Zone. You'll be asked to confirm your password, then your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Without the app installed

If you no longer have the app and want your account deleted, email us from the address registered on your account and we'll process the request.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (username, email, role);
  • Every session note you authored;
  • Your membership in your party;
  • Your login credentials.

Party Bank items you contributed remain as part of your former party's shared inventory, since they're shared party data rather than personal data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are processed within 30 days of receipt.
