Privacy Policy

Session Journal — last updated September 8, 2026

Session Journal is a companion app for tabletop RPG players, published by Dungeons Not Dating ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can delete it.

Short version: we collect only what's needed to run your account and your party's shared notes and inventory — your email, a username, and the content you write. We don't run ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone outside the infrastructure that runs the app (Google Firebase).

Information we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address, password (handled entirely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store it in plain form), username, and role (Player or Game Master).
  • Party information: which party you belong to, and the invite code used to join or create it.
  • Session notes you write: campaign title, session date, notes, memorable moments, notable NPCs and places, and who you've chosen to share each note with.
  • Party Bank items: inventory entries (item name, description, quantity, value) added by you or your party members.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, or any advertising identifier. The app does not include analytics or tracking software of any kind.

How your content is shared

Session notes are visible according to the setting you choose when you write them:

  • Private — visible only to you.
  • Whole Party — visible to every member of your party.
  • Someone specific — visible to you and whichever party members you select.

Party Bank inventory is visible to every member of that party. Parties are closed groups — someone can only join with an invite code a Game Master shares with them directly; there is no public discovery of users, parties, or notes.

How we use and store information

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as our infrastructure provider. We use your information solely to operate the app's features described above — account login, displaying your notes and your party's shared content, and party inventory. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to any third party.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Account → Delete My Account inside the app. Doing so:

  • Permanently deletes every session note you authored;
  • Permanently deletes your account profile (username, email, role);
  • Removes you from your party's member list;
  • Permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication login credentials.

Party Bank items you contributed remain as part of your former party's shared inventory, since they're shared party data rather than personal data tied to your account. If you no longer have access to the app, see our account deletion request page for an alternative way to request deletion.

Children's privacy

Session Journal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at the address below and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
